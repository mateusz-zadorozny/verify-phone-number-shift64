# D02 — Keep the GitHub self-updater as it is and find out at WordPress.org review

- Date, owner: 2026-09-22, Mateusz Zadorożny (maintainer)
- Context and the options weighed: `src/Admin/GitHubUpdater.php` queries `api.github.com` so the plugin can update itself from GitHub releases. The stated destination is the WordPress.org plugin directory, where updates are served by WordPress.org. Three options were put to the maintainer: remove the updater at submission; keep it but disable it when the plugin came from the directory; or keep it as is and learn the outcome at review. No recommendation was offered, because the exact current WordPress.org guideline on bundled self-updaters was not established in this session.
- Decision and why: keep it as is and find out at review. Chosen by the maintainer without a recommendation from the agent.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: a submission may come back with a change request, costing a review round. Until submission, installations from the release ZIP keep one-click updates. Revisit on the first review response, or if the guideline is checked and turns out to forbid it.
- Status: active
